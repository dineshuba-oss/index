Meet the founder

The experience behind AnveX TechnoLab.

Dinesh Prasad Bhatt

Founder, AnveX TechnoLab

MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning) and ICT professional with more than 16 years of experience across UN agencies and development organizations, including UNHCR, WFP, Heifer Project International, SAC Nepal and NIDR.

Dinesh combines field research (key informant interviews, focus groups, baseline and endline surveys, data management) with hands-on ICT support and training. AnveX TechnoLab brings that mix of evidence and technology to organizations and learners across Sudurpaschim.

Recognition: UNHCR Nepal presented Dinesh with an Appreciation of Service award in December 2020 for more than 10 years of dedicated service.
